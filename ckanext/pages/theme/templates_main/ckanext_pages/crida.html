{{ _('What is CRIDA?') }}

{{ _('CRIDA (Climate Risk Informed Decision Analysis) is a collaborative methodology developed by UNESCO, ICIWaRM, and partners to support robust water management decisions under climate uncertainty. Rather than relying on single climate projections, CRIDA uses bottom-up vulnerability assessments, stress testing, and adaptive planning pathways to identify decisions that perform well across a wide range of possible futures.') }}

{{ _('Since its inception, CRIDA has been recognized by the World Bank as an A+ method for developing resilience projects in the water sector and acknowledged by the Green Climate Fund (GCF) for adaptive planning in water project design.') }}
